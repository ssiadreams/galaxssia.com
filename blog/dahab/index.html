Dahab — where I felt complete stillness

Dahab doesn't try to impress you. That's exactly why it heals you.

After Mauritius, I needed a place that asked nothing of me. I found it there — between the desert and the Red Sea, where time moves differently and no one is in a hurry. I stayed. I breathed. For the first time in a long while, the quiet around me matched the quiet inside.

I filmed there, too. Something I'm still editing — it'll become a video essay about silence and coming back. I'll leave it here when it's done.
