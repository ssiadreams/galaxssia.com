Cluj-Napoca — the three years that shaped me

Before I knew what I wanted to become, Cluj showed me who I was.

Three years there, studying Film, in a city that breathes culture without bragging about it. Cluj taught me how to look — and it gave me TIFF, the festival that shaped me more than any course ever did. Ten days a year when the whole city becomes one enormous cinema, and you finally understand why you chose this road.

Places I still miss — cafés, Cinema Victoria, Cetățuia at sunset, the river, the bookshops. Each one with a story. I'll add them, one by one.
