Mauritius — the dark night of the soul

Everyone thinks paradise heals you. Mauritius broke me open.

I arrived with everything I didn't want to feel packed in my luggage. And the island, instead of distracting me, gave me exactly what I was running from: quiet. Too much of it. The kind where there's nowhere left to hide from yourself.

It was the dark night of the soul, turquoise ocean on every side. Perfect irony. But there, in the most beautiful place I'd ever been, I woke up. I saw clearly what I'd built out of fear — and what I was about to tear down.

I didn't come back rested. I came back awake.
