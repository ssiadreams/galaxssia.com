About · a letter

For years, the work and the wandering lived apart. One held a camera. The other packed a bag. Galaxssia is where they became the same thing.

A film studio, a marketing atelier, and a travel signature under one sky — built on a simple belief: a place, a story, and a brand are told the same way. Through light. Through rhythm. Through truth.

The road here was long. A decade of searching — through films, through poetry, through long nights of questions. A loss that cut deep and left behind empathy, and that restlessness for reading the shadows people hide. Film became a way of decoding life. Storytelling, a way of surviving it.

When the walls of the industry began to feel too rigid, this space appeared — a bridge between art and stillness, poetry and productivity, creation and chaos. Here, creation isn't always cinematic. Sometimes it's a campaign. Sometimes a journey. Sometimes a sentence written at 3 a.m.

Galaxssia makes films. It builds brands that are seen and felt. It takes people to places where history and cinema still breathe. And in everything it touches, it looks for the same thing: something you stop — and stay — to watch.

Services

Three crafts, one instinct

Whether it's a film, a brand, or a journey, the work begins the same way — with a story worth telling, and the discipline to tell it well.

Film Production

From first idea to final cut

Development, production, and post — short films, documentaries, branded films, and music videos. The same eye that serves art serves the work: story first, image always, nothing wasted. Galaxssia produces films that feel like something, and delivers them ready for the screen they were made for.

Branding & Marketing

A brand isn't a logo. It's how you're remembered.

Galaxssia builds the whole arc — strategy, story, and the machinery that carries it: SEO and GEO, Google Analytics, Meta and Google Ads, full campaign management. And because story is the point, the content is made here too — filmed, edited, and delivered ready to post, guided by a content calendar that keeps the voice consistent and the audience growing. Insight becomes content. Content becomes reach.

Workshops & Creative Retreats

Craft, taught the way it's lived

Small groups, real work, and space to think — workshops on filmmaking, storytelling, and content, and retreats that take creatives somewhere beautiful enough to remember why they started. Part masterclass, part reset. You leave with work in hand and clarity in your chest.
