Studio

The house & its departments

Where curiosity becomes work. The Studio is the working heart of Galaxssia: several creative disciplines under one roof, each starting from story and ending in something finished and beautiful. Scattered into projects, made to travel internationally.

Film & Production

A producer's eye behind every frame

Film, documentary, advertising and brand video — from the first idea to ready-to-publish material. We shoot the way we see: natural light, real faces and places, the patience of cinema.

Creative & Marketing Services

We treat a brand like a film

Brand strategy, content and campaigns for businesses that want to be found and remembered. A clear vision, a story well told, and the discipline to finish it. Packages built around outcomes, not task-lists.
