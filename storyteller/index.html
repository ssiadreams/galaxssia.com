Who I am

Ssia Cremenescu

A storyteller shaped by film and by travel, curious about the world and determined to be kind. She speaks five languages, has begun again more than once, and believes we are all artists — some of us just forgot.

Galaxssia grew from that belief: a place to merge the two things that shaped her, travel and cinema, so curiosity has somewhere to go. If you're curious about the world, you already belong here.

A letter to the world

“The world is not a list of places: it is a way of paying attention. Come and live like the main character. It's your story. Begin it out loud.”

— Ssia
