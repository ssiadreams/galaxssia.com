Journeys

We don't sell destinations. We follow stories.

Every Galaxssia journey begins with a film or a fragment of history — and takes you to where it actually happened. In 2027, three departures. Few travelers. Many stories.

2027 · on the trail of history & film

Three departures

Egypt · August 2027

Egypt of the pharaohs

Where the oldest stories were carved in stone. Egypt is cinema before cinema — temples, tombs, and a river that has been the main character for five thousand years. We walk it the way a film reads a location: slowly, deliberately, looking for what the postcards miss.

Greece · May 2027

Greece of myth & light

Myth, light, and the sea that started everything. Greece is where storytelling was born — and where the light itself feels directed. A journey through history and the landscapes that shaped how the West sees beauty.

Caribbean · November 2027

Caribbean cruise

Adventure, on open water. A cruise through the islands where history, myth, and cinema meet the horizon. Movement as meditation — the kind of journey that rearranges something in you by the time you dock.
