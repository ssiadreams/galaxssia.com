Cultural Travel · Art & Travel Therapy

We don't sell destinations. We follow stories — and we heal along the way.

Galaxssia Journeys is travel as therapy. Each journey moves through a country's inner soul — its history, its cinema, its silence — and gives you the time to actually feel it. We heal by watching films and debating them under open skies, by swimming, by practising yoga, by walking through ancient places, by letting a culture rearrange something in us. Every trip includes massage and room to rest. Because a journey should return you to yourself, not exhaust you.

How we travel

We stay longer

Five days is never enough — by the fifth, you've only just begun to belong. So every journey (city breaks aside) runs a minimum of seven nights, most of them closer to ten. Long enough for the place to stop being scenery and start becoming an experience.

We travel through four doors — cinema, wellness, spirituality, and healing. We watch and discuss films tied to where we are. We move our bodies — sea, yoga, walking. We visit the sacred and the ancient. And we leave lighter than we arrived.

Small groups. Real immersion. Nothing rushed.

Three flagship journeys

Greece · 2027

Myth, Light & the Sea

10 nights · 7 islands · 1–2 nights each

The birthplace of storytelling, island by island. Ten nights across seven islands we believe you must see — each one a different light, a different myth, a different rhythm. Swimming, ancient sites, films under the stars, and the slow blue calm that only the Aegean gives. This is Greece felt, not photographed.

Morocco · 2027

Between Desert & Sea

8 nights · full country · beach + sightseeing + rest

Eight nights across the whole of Morocco — days for the sea, days for the medinas and the history, days for doing nothing at all. Timed around a rare sky: we're in Tangier on 1–2 August for the total solar eclipse of 2 August 2027, one of the great celestial events of the decade. Optional extension: cross the Strait through Gibraltar into Spain for 2 nights.

Egypt · 2027

The Oldest Story Ever Told

18 days · deepest immersion

Eighteen days along the river that has been the main character for five thousand years. Temples, tombs, the desert, the Nile — Egypt is cinema before cinema. This is our deepest immersion: history, spirituality, and stillness, taken at the pace such a place deserves.
