The story

From what I love, to a profession

I started from a love of story and image, and turned it into a craft: film production. I studied Film at Babeș-Bolyai University and completed an MA in Film Production at UNATC, producing Romanian short films that travelled to festivals.

I speak 7 languages (English, Romanian native, Italian, advanced Spanish, plus German, French, Portuguese) — a tool that opens collaborations and stories beyond borders.

With Galaxssia, I bring a producer's discipline into the world of brands: clear vision, structured execution, attention to every frame. I believe the best businesses, like the best films, are built on a story told well.
